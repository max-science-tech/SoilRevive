## 👤 SoilRevive User Simulation

### Scenario 1 — Curry Leaf Rescue

#### Step 1: User opens SoilRevive

**Plant Selected:** Curry Patta

**Location:** Outdoor

**Pot Size:** Medium

---

#### Step 2: Symptom Check

* ☑ Leaves are yellow
* ☑ Soil is very wet
* ☑ Fungus is visible
* ☐ Leaves are drooping

---

#### Step 3: Diagnosis Engine

**Possible Cause:** Overwatering

**Confidence:** High

---

#### Step 4: Soil Health Score

| Parameter        | Score |
| ---------------- | ----- |
| Moisture         | 5     |
| Drainage         | 10    |
| Leaf Health      | 10    |
| Fungal Presence  | 8     |
| Growth Condition | 10    |

**Total = 43/100**

**Status:** 🚨 Poor

---

#### Step 5: Emergency Remedy

* Stop watering for 2–3 days
* Keep the plant in bright indirect sunlight
* Remove fungal growth from the soil surface
* Improve airflow around the pot
* Check that drainage holes are not blocked

---

#### Step 6: Recovery Prediction

**Expected Improvement:** 2–3 weeks

**Estimated New Score:** 65–75/100
