# 🌱 SoilRevive Emergency Remedies

## Overwatering
- Stop watering for 2–3 days
- Keep the plant in bright indirect sunlight
- Sprinkle cinnamon on affected soil or roots

## Underwatering
- Water deeply until excess drains out
- Soak the pot for 10–20 minutes if soil is extremely dry

## Fungal Growth
- Remove the top 1–2 cm of soil
- Replace with fresh dry soil
- Increase airflow around the plant

## Algae on Soil
- Scrape off the green layer
- Add a thin layer of dry sand or fresh soil
- Reduce watering frequency

## Quick Nutrient Boost
- 1 tsp Epsom salt in 1 liter water (once a month)
- Add a small amount of compost or vermicompost3
