## 📱 SoilRevive UI Flow (Phase 1 MVP)

### Screen 1 — Splash Screen

**Purpose:** Introduce the app.

Display:

* 🌱 SoilRevive
* *Reviving Soil, Sustaining Life*
* Loading animation (future feature)

---

### Screen 2 — Home Screen

**Purpose:** Start plant diagnosis.

Components:

* Plant dropdown
* Indoor / Outdoor option
* Pot size option
* Button: **Check Plant Health**

**Next:** Symptom Checker

---

### Screen 3 — Symptom Checker

**Questions**

* Are the leaves yellow?
* Are the leaves drooping?
* Is the soil wet or dry?
* Is fungus visible on the soil?
* Is the stem soft or firm?

**Next:** Diagnosis Engine

---

### Screen 4 — Diagnosis Result

Display:

* Probable Cause
* Confidence Level
* Short Explanation

Example:

**Possible Cause:** Overwatering

**Confidence:** High

**Reason:** Wet soil + yellow leaves + fungal growth.

**Next:** Soil Health Score

---

### Screen 5 — Soil Health Score

Display:

* Moisture Score
* Drainage Score
* Leaf Health Score
* Fungal Status Score
* Growth Condition Score

**Overall:** 43/100

**Status:** 🚨 Poor

**Next:** Emergency Remedies

---

### Screen 6 — Emergency Remedies

Show:

* Stop watering for 2–3 days
* Improve airflow
* Remove fungal growth
* Check drainage holes

Buttons:

* **Save to Plant Record**
* **Check Again**

---

### Future Screens

* Soil Passport
* Recovery Tracker
* Community Soil Library
* Green Impact Leaderboard
* Weather Alerts

These screens belong to **future phases** and are not required for the MVP.

---

## MVP User Journey

Splash → Home → Symptoms → Diagnosis → Score → Remedies

This flow represents the **minimum viable user experience** for SoilRevive Phase 1.
