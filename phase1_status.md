## 🏁 Current MVP Capabilities

A user can:

1. Enter a plant name
2. Press **Check Soil Health** to receive general care advice
3. Select symptoms using checkboxes
4. Press **Plant Doctor** to receive:

   * A possible diagnosis
   * A short explanation
   * A Soil Health Score
