# 🤖 SoilRevive Diagnosis Engine

## Rule 1: Overwatering
IF
- Soil is wet
- Leaves are yellow
- Fungus or algae is visible

THEN
- Possible Cause: Overwatering
- Confidence: High
- Action: Stop watering for 2–3 days and improve airflow.

---

## Rule 2: Underwatering
IF
- Soil is dry
- Leaf edges are brown and crispy
- Plant looks wilted

THEN
- Possible Cause: Underwatering
- Confidence: High
- Action: Deep water the plant until excess water drains out.

---

## Rule 3: Poor Drainage
IF
- Soil remains wet for many days
- Pot feels heavy
- Water drains very slowly

THEN
- Possible Cause: Poor Drainage
- Confidence: Medium
- Action: Loosen the soil and add perlite, coarse sand, or cocopeat.

---

## Rule 4: Early Root Rot
IF
- Soil smells sour or musty
- Roots are brown and soft
- Plant wilts even when soil is wet

THEN
- Possible Cause: Early Root Rot
- Confidence: High
- Action: Trim damaged roots, dust with cinnamon, and repot into fresh well-draining soil.

---

## Rule 5: Possible Nutrient Deficiency
IF
- Leaves are pale
- Growth is slow
- Soil moisture appears normal

THEN
- Possible Cause: Nutrient Deficiency
- Confidence: Medium
- Action: Add compost or a balanced organic fertilizer.
