# 📊 SoilRevive Soil Health Score System

## Purpose
The Soil Health Score gives a simple estimate of how healthy the soil is on a scale of **0–100**.

---

## Scoring Categories

### 1. Moisture Condition (20 points)
- Soil moist but not soggy → 20
- Slightly dry or slightly wet → 15
- Very dry or very wet → 5
- Waterlogged → 0

---

### 2. Drainage Quality (20 points)
- Water drains quickly → 20
- Drains slowly → 10
- Water remains pooled → 0

---

### 3. Leaf Health (25 points)
- Green healthy leaves → 25
- Minor yellowing → 18
- Moderate yellowing → 10
- Severe yellowing or leaf drop → 0

---

### 4. Fungal or Algae Presence (15 points)
- No fungus or algae → 15
- Small amount visible → 8
- Heavy fungal growth → 0

---

### 5. Growth Condition (20 points)
- Active new growth → 20
- Slow growth → 10
- No new growth → 5
- Plant declining → 0

---

## Final Formula

Soil Health Score =
Moisture + Drainage + Leaf Health + Fungal Status + Growth Condition

Maximum Score = **100**

---

## Score Interpretation

| Score | Condition |
|------|-----------|
| 85–100 | 🌿 Excellent |
| 70–84 | 🌱 Good |
| 50–69 | ⚠ Needs Attention |
| 30–49 | 🚨 Poor |
| 0–29 | ❌ Critical |

---

## Example

### Curry Leaf Example
- Moisture: 15
- Drainage: 10
- Leaf Health: 18
- Fungal Presence: 8
- Growth Condition: 10

**Total = 61/100**

**Result:** ⚠ Needs Attention

**Suggested Action:** Improve drainage, reduce watering frequency, remove fungal growth, and monitor new leaf growth for one week.
