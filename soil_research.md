# 🌱 Soil Research Notes

## Healthy Soil
- Loose and airy
- Drains excess water within a few seconds
- Contains organic matter such as compost or cocopeat
- Does not have a bad smell

## Overwatering Signs
- Yellow leaves
- Drooping leaves even when soil is wet
- Fungus or algae on the soil surface
- Soil stays wet for many days
- Roots may become brown and soft

## Underwatering Signs
- Dry soil
- Brown crispy leaf edges
- Wilting that improves after watering
- Soil pulls away from the edges of the pot

## Poor Drainage Signs
- Water remains on the surface for a long time
- Soil feels compact and heavy
- Pot has blocked or insufficient drainage holes
- Roots receive less oxygen

## Fungal Growth Signs
- White, gray, or green fuzzy growth on soil
- Musty or sour smell
- Dark soft roots
- Wilting despite wet soil

## Quick Home Remedies
- Cinnamon for mild fungal problems
- Diluted hydrogen peroxide (1 part 3% peroxide + 4 parts water) for oxygenating roots
- Neem oil spray for fungal leaf spots
- Compost or vermicompost for nutrient improvement
- Epsom salt (1 tsp per liter) for possible magnesium deficiency

## Plant-Specific Notes

### Curry Leaf (Currypatta)
- Sensitive to waterlogged soil
- Needs good drainage and plenty of sunlight
- Yellowing often indicates overwatering or nutrient deficiency

### Tulsi (Holy Basil)
- Prefers evenly moist but not soggy soil
- Good airflow helps prevent fungal problems
- Nutrient stress may reduce leaf aroma and growth

### Money Plant (Pothos)
- Tolerates slightly dry soil better than wet soil
- Yellow lower leaves often suggest overwatering
- Brown crispy tips may suggest underwatering or low humidity
